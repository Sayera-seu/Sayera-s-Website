About me

I was born and raised in Dhaka, and I have lived here with my parents and two siblings for as long as I can remember. School here, college here, and now university here too.

I chose software engineering because I want to build things, not just study them. Right now I am at the very start of that road: first year, learning the fundamentals, writing small programs, and slowly getting better at turning an idea into working code. The goal is a long one — to become a great software engineer — so I am treating every semester as a step toward it.
